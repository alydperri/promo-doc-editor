# Leveling Guide (Template)

This file is a template. Replace the examples below with your organization's actual leveling expectations, role guidelines, or career ladder definitions. The editor uses these to evaluate whether promotion documents assess the employee against the right criteria for the proposed level.

**If you don't fill this in, the editor still works.** It will assess writing quality, specificity, objectivity, and structure — it just won't evaluate criteria alignment against your specific leveling framework (Rules R3, R7, and R13 will note that no leveling guide was available).

---

## How to use this file

For the role and level relevant to the promotion you're writing, fill in:
- **Role:** The job family or role type (e.g., Software Engineer, Product Manager, Designer)
- **Current level:** The employee's current level
- **Proposed level:** The level being proposed for promotion
- **Key expectations at the proposed level:** What success looks like at the next level — scope, complexity, influence, impact, technical/functional depth
