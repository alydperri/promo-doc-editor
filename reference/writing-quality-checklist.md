# Writing Quality Checklist

Use this checklist to evaluate any section of a promotion document or standalone feedback. Each quality maps to specific rules in `rubric.md`.

---

## Balanced (R4, R17)

- [ ] Does the assessment include both strengths and weaknesses?
- [ ] Are negative details or misses included when relevant, not omitted?
- [ ] For standalone feedback: are both Reasons to Promote and Reasons Not to Promote present?
- [ ] Is the overall tone honest rather than promotional?

## Objective (R5, R12)

- [ ] Are claims supported by specific examples and data, not just opinions?
- [ ] Is the writing free of exaggerated language ("flawless," "amazing," "practically perfect")?
- [ ] Does it describe performance, not personality?
- [ ] Could a skeptical reviewer accept every statement based on the evidence provided?

## Values-aligned (R6)

- [ ] Are performance examples connected to the organization's shared values/principles?
- [ ] Is the values vocabulary used accurately, not just name-dropped?
- [ ] (If no company values provided: note this dimension was not assessed)

## Criteria-focused (R3, R7, R13)

- [ ] Does the writing evaluate against next-level expectations, not current-level performance?
- [ ] Are the specific criteria for the proposed level and role referenced?
- [ ] Is tenure or time-in-role treated as a valid criterion? (It shouldn't be.)
- [ ] (If no leveling guide provided: note this dimension was not assessed)

## Specific (R8, R14)

- [ ] Does each example answer: What was the situation? What did the person do? What was the result?
- [ ] Are impact claims backed by specific evidence — data where available, or detailed qualitative outcomes where not?
- [ ] Is the employee's individual contribution distinguished from the team's achievement?
- [ ] Are growth areas described with the same level of detail as strengths?

## Stands on its own (R9)

- [ ] Are acronyms spelled out on first use?
- [ ] Is jargon avoided or explained?
- [ ] Could a reviewer from a different org understand the impact without additional context?
- [ ] Are project names, tools, or internal references accompanied by brief descriptions?

## Clear and concise (R10, W1-W5)

- [ ] Is the writing easy to read on first pass?
- [ ] Does each paragraph have a clear topic sentence?
- [ ] Is active voice used by default?
- [ ] Has filler language been cut?
- [ ] Is the formatting clean and purposeful (not decorative)?

## Growth areas completeness (R11, R15, R16)

- [ ] Are growth areas present? (Their absence is a rejection-level issue.)
- [ ] Does each growth area include a plan for how the gap is being closed?
- [ ] Has relevant promotion feedback been acknowledged and incorporated?
- [ ] Are feedback trends across multiple providers addressed?
- [ ] Is conflicting feedback explained rather than dismissed?
